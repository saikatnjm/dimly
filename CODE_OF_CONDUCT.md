# Code of conduct

Dimly follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and respectful, assume good intent, welcome people of every
background and experience level, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment,
insults and personal attacks aren't accepted in issues, pull requests or
anywhere else the project lives.

If something goes wrong, contact the maintainer privately through
[GitHub](https://github.com/saikatnjm). Reports are handled confidentially.
